Reviewers of the `claude-structured` kind may give each finding an optional `category` slug, and an optional `guidance` section of `config.yaml` (`recurrence_threshold` and `window_days`, both required) turns recurrence into a suggestion: when that many findings of one category in one repository were fixed within the window and after the last suggestion, autoreview sends one `guidance_suggested` notification (default level `digest`, routable like any event) that names the repository, the category and the counted findings with their text, so a rule for the repository's `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md` can be written. autoreview writes to no repository. The shipped reviewer prompts ask for the category and for a note in the summary when a change makes the agent guidance files inaccurate. Without the section nothing is counted or sent.
